\## 🛒 Procure-to-Pay (PTP) Workflow



The Procure-to-Pay cycle demonstrates a complete purchasing process executed within SAP S/4HANA Cloud:



1\. \*\*Manage Purchase Orders\*\* — Accessing the main Fiori purchasing dashboard.

2\. \*\*Create Purchase Order\*\* — Entering supplier details, company codes, and line items.

3\. \*\*Purchase Order Created\*\* — System confirmation toast notification for PO creation.

4\. \*\*Post Goods Receipt\*\* — Entering details for receiving goods against the PO.

5\. \*\*Goods Receipt Posted\*\* — Material selection (`MZ-FG-C900`) and document verification.

6\. \*\*Create Supplier Invoice\*\* — Invoice entry against the received goods.

7\. \*\*Supplier Invoice Posted\*\* — Successful posting of supplier invoice (`5105665819`).
