- Material master exploration

- Organizational structure documentation

- Purchase order processing and monitoring
- Purchase contract exploration
- Goods receipt processing
- Inventory and stock monitoring
- Material document analysis
- Supplier invoice processing
- Invoice follow-on accounting document analysis
- MM–FI integration analysis
- Purchasing monitoring and analytics documentation

## 4. Implementation Approach

The project focuses on understanding and demonstrating standard SAP business processes rather than claiming a production enterprise implementation.

Where the SAP trial environment limited configuration or advanced functionality, the relevant concept was documented as a future enhancement.

## 5. Enterprise Future Enhancements

- Additional inventory KPIs
