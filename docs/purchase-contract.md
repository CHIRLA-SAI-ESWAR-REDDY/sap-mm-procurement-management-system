\# Purchase Contract Exploration



\## Objective



To explore the Purchase Contract functionality available in SAP S/4HANA Cloud Public Edition and understand how Purchase Orders relate to outline agreements.



\---



\## SAP Application Used



\- Display Purchase Contract

\- Manage Purchase Orders



\---



\## Activities Performed



\### 1. Opened Display Purchase Contract



The Display Purchase Contract application was launched from the SAP Fiori Launchpad.



Screenshot:



\- 22\_Display\_Purchase\_Contract\_Initial\_Screen.png



\---



\### 2. Used Search Help



The search help was opened to search for available agreement numbers.



Screenshot:



\- 23\_Purchase\_Contract\_Search\_Help.png



\---



\### 3. Reviewed Available Documents



The search results displayed purchasing documents available in the trial environment.



During exploration, the available documents were Purchase Orders rather than Purchase Contracts.



Screenshot:



\- 24\_Purchase\_Contract\_List.png



\---



\### Trial System Observation



The SAP S/4HANA Cloud Public Edition trial did not contain accessible Purchase Contract master data.



Instead, the available search results referenced Purchase Orders. Therefore, Purchase Contract header and item details could not be displayed.
