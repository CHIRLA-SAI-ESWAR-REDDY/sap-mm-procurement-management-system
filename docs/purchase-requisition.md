# Purchase Requisition Management

## Overview

A Purchase Requisition (PR) is an internal document used to request the procurement of materials or services. It is the first step in the SAP MM Procure-to-Pay (P2P) process.

## Objective

The objective of this exercise was to explore existing Purchase Requisitions in SAP S/4HANA Cloud and understand the information maintained within a requisition.

## Activities Performed

- Opened Display Purchase Requisition - Advanced
- Accessed the Purchase Requisition selection dialog
- Used SAP Search Help to locate existing Purchase Requisitions
- Viewed the list of available Purchase Requisitions
- Opened Purchase Requisition 10000091
- Reviewed Material Data including Material, Quantity, Plant, Delivery Date, Material Group and Account Assignment
