# SAP MM Procurement Management System – Business Scenario & Implementation Case Study

## 1. Business Context

Organizations regularly purchase materials, equipment, and services from external suppliers.

Without an integrated procurement process, organizations may face:

- Poor visibility of purchasing requirements
- Difficulty tracking procurement documents
- Limited visibility of goods receipts
- Inventory tracking issues
- Difficulty connecting supplier invoices with procurement transactions
- Limited procurement reporting

This project demonstrates how SAP S/4HANA Cloud Public Edition can support an integrated procurement lifecycle using SAP Materials Management (MM).

---

## 2. Business Problem

The project models a controlled procurement process in which a purchasing requirement can move through procurement, receiving, inventory, invoice processing, and financial accounting.

The main business objectives are:

- Centralize procurement information
- Improve document traceability
- Connect purchasing with inventory
- Connect supplier invoices with procurement documents
- Provide procurement monitoring capabilities
- Provide purchasing analysis and reporting

---

## 3. Proposed SAP Solution

The solution uses:

**SAP S/4HANA Cloud Public Edition**

with:

**SAP Materials Management (MM)**

The project covers:

- Supplier / Vendor Master
- Material Master
- Organizational Structure
- Purchase Requisitions
- Purchase Orders
- Purchase Contracts
- Goods Receipts
- Inventory Management
- Supplier Invoice Processing
- Accounting Follow-on Documents
- Purchase Requisition Monitoring
- Purchase Order Monitoring
- Purchasing Analytics and Reporting
